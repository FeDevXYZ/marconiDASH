Load average: what is it?

Load Average shows how busy your system is. It measures the average number of processes running or waiting for CPU resources over three time periods: 1, 5 and 15 minutes.

For example, on a quad-core CPU:

  • 1.00 – Low load.
  • 2.00 – About half CPU capacity.
  • 4.00 – CPU fully utilized.
  • 8.00 – High load, with processes waiting for CPU time.

Note: High Load Average doesn't always mean high CPU usage. Processes waiting for disk or other resources can also increase it.
